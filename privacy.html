Privacy Policy

Verasol Archive Uploader, an internal tool of VerasolStudio. Effective 9 October 2026.

What it accesses

Through the Google Drive permission drive.file, only the files and folders that Verasol Archive Uploader created. It requests no other Google data: no other Drive files, and no Gmail, contacts, calendar, or profile information.

How it uses the data

To create its archive folder, upload files, read back each file's size and checksum to confirm the copy, and delete files when their retention period ends.

Where the data is stored

The files stay in the Google Drive of the Google account that authorized the tool. The access token that lets the tool reach them is stored encrypted on a server operated by VerasolStudio and is not stored anywhere else.

Sharing

VerasolStudio does not sell this data or transfer it outside VerasolStudio, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Verasol Archive Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner of the Google account can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in Google Drive remain there until they are deleted.

Contact

Use the support email address shown on the Google consent screen for this app.
